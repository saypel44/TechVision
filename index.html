About Tech Vision

Tech Vision is a Bhutan-based Digital Art and Edu-Tech startup specializing in AI-powered educational content, illustration services, and creative digital solutions. Founded by Chador Wangchuk, a former art teacher, the company focuses on transforming traditional learning through visual storytelling and artificial intelligence.

Our expertise includes educational illustration, graphic design, AI-driven learning materials, and digital content development for schools, educators, and global clients. Tech Vision works closely with educational institutions and creative partners to develop engaging, accessible, and visually rich learning experiences.

Driven by innovation, creativity, and inclusive education, Tech Vision aims to bridge learning gaps by supporting different learning styles through technology and design.

Our Vision


To inspire children's learning and creativity through innovative visual storytelling that blends art and technology.

Our Mission


To deliver AI-powered Edu-Tech solutions that simplify complex learning through personalized visual guides, while providing 24/7 global design and illustration services that blend technology with human creativity.

Global Reach


To date, we have worked with a diverse range of clients, including schools, colleges, educators, international organizations, and authors.
